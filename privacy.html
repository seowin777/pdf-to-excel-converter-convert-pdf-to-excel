PDF to Excel Converter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What the extension does with your data

The PDF files you open are processed only in the memory of the extension's side panel on your computer. Nothing is sent anywhere: no files, no text, no tables, no analytics.

The only network request the extension makes is reading the PDF of the tab on which you clicked its icon, from that tab's own address, at the moment of your click.

The password of a protected PDF is kept in memory only and is never saved.

Your choices (sheets, file format, numbers option), a flag that hides the first-run hint and the state of the rating prompt are saved in your browser (chrome.storage.local). The content of your PDFs is never saved.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Website analytics

The welcome and goodbye pages on this website use Google Analytics to count installs and removals. The extension itself contains no analytics code.
